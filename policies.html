Policies

All attending team members must abide by the university-wide policies below. In addition, please designate a member to act as your team’s default emergency contact.

Sexual Assault Policy (SAP)

Bucky Noh Dhol (BND) board is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment for everyone. We aim to be trauma-informed and under no circumstances tolerate sexual assault and harassment of any kind. It is mandatory for BND members to report any incidents to directors and/or risk management chairs. Based on the consent of the survivor, details may be omitted in the report. Any reported incidents will be taken seriously and addressed promptly. Following a thorough investigation, disciplinary action will be taken as necessary to ensure the safety and wellbeing of everyone involved. This goes for all participating members of Bucky Noh Dhol, including all attending members of the team, executive board, liaisons, and event managers.

Alcohol & Other Drugs (AOD) Policy

Bucky Noh Dhol (BND) strictly adheres to state, university, and venue policies regarding alcohol and drug use. Consumption or possession of illegal substances during any BND-related activity is strictly prohibited. Members are expected to make responsible decisions and report any known incidents involving substance misuse to the directors and/or risk management chairs. All reports will be taken seriously, and appropriate disciplinary action will be taken to uphold the safety and trust of our organization.

Gun Violence Policy

Bucky Noh Dhol (BND) has zero-tolerance for firearms. In accordance with the University of Wisconsin System administrative code, no competition attendee “may carry, possess or use any dangerous weapon on university lands or in university buildings or facilities”. BND grieves the tragic incidents of gun violence at all South Asian competitions. In that spirit, we ask that teams remain together and with their liaisons throughout the competition. In addition to that, we ask that team members be escorted to and from the AP venue by a Bucky Noh Dhol board member. If any member feels unsafe, immediately contact BND directors and/or risk management chairs for assistance. For survivor resources, please speak to a BND member, visit the Raas All-Stars website, or utilize the University of Wisconsin–Madison’s services.

UW-Madison students are not designated reporters, but we provide resources for reporting and are fully committed to supporting anyone who requests it.
